How it works

Three steps. Nothing lost in translation.

01

Say it your way.

Words, a rough sketch, or an old tech pack. Whatever you already have.

02

Answer what you forgot.

Before anything goes out, NEIRA asks for the missing details. The ones that cause wrong samples.

03

Factories quote.

They get your brief in their own words and send a price. You choose.
