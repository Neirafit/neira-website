ABOUT NEIRA

Fashion meets
infrastructure.

NEIRA was born from a simple frustration: the gap between what a designer imagines and what a factory receives. Miscommunication, failed samples, endless back-and-forth — every fashion brand knows this pain.

We built NEIRA to be the technical layer that closes this gap. Not another design tool. Not another PLM. The missing infrastructure between creative intent and factory execution.

Based in Seoul, at the intersection of fashion manufacturing and technology — where the problem is most real.
